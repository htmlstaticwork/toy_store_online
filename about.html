🌿 Our Story

Crafting Joy Through Learning

We believe every child deserves toys that are safe, sustainable, and designed to unlock their full potential. That's why we started ToyWorld.

🎯 Our Mission

Safe & Educational Toys 🌿

At ToyWorld, our mission is simple: to create toys that are as good for children as they are for the planet. We combine time-tested wooden toy craftsmanship with modern educational design principles.

Every product in our collection is independently tested and certified safe, made from sustainably harvested wood, and finished with non-toxic, water-based paints. We believe that the best toys are those that stimulate a child's natural curiosity while being gentle on the environment.

📅 Our Journey

Brand Story Timeline

2018

The Spark

Founded by two parents who couldn't find quality wooden toys that were both educational and eco-friendly.

2019

First Collection

Launched our first collection of 25 handcrafted wooden toys, sold exclusively at local markets.

2021

Going Online

Launched ToyWorld.com and expanded to serve families across the country with over 200 products.

2023

Global Reach

Expanded internationally to 25+ countries. Introduced Montessori-certified toy line.

2026

Today

Serving 50,000+ families with 500+ products, all crafted with the same love and dedication as day one.

♻️ Sustainability

Materials & Sustainability

Every detail matters — from the wood we source to the packaging we use.

FSC Certified Wood

All our wood is sourced from FSC-certified forests, ensuring responsible forestry practices and sustainability.

Non-Toxic Finishes

Water-based paints and finishes that are free from lead, phthalates, and BPA. Safe for even the youngest hands and mouths.

Zero-Waste Packaging

100% recyclable packaging made from recycled cardboard and soy ink. No plastics, no styrofoam, no waste.
